Our Story

Founded by the Johnson family in 1985, Sweet Haven Bakery began as a small kitchen with big dreams. What started as a passion for creating delicious breads has grown into a beloved community institution.

Today, we continue to use the same time-honored recipes and techniques passed down through generations, ensuring every product carries the warmth and love that makes our bakery special.
